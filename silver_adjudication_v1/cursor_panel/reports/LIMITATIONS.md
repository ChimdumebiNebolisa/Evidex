# Cursor panel limitations

- **One model family.** All five judges and the resolver used
  `cursor-grok-4.6-high-fast`. Within-Cursor-panel agreement is not
  independent human validation and is not cross-family validation.
- **GLM is a replication panel only.** Frozen GLM-5.3 Stage A is compared
  to Cursor Stage A. Partial GLM Stage B never enters Cursor A→B→C
  analyses and is not a completed GLM progressive-disclosure panel.
- **Same-family resolver.** The resolver used the same locked model as the
  judges, with anonymized `r1`–`r5` labels. It can correct isolated judge
  errors; it cannot supply an independent warrant.
- **Provider filter.** `SA-000352` is excluded (judged N = 1,060 of 1,061).
  Unblinding joins 1,060 items.
- **Stage C evidence.** Structured evidence is reconstructed from the
  existing FEVER wiki archive. Two pointer-only archive gaps remain as
  already reconstructed. Judges did not browse Wikipedia or retrieve new
  pages.
- **Taxonomy is derived, not gold.** `silver_*` labels are rule-based
  summaries of consensus transitions. They are cautious diagnostics, not
  human ground truth.
- **Blinding does not remove world knowledge.** Isolated packets hide
  FEVER gold, GPT predictions, cohort labels, NLI, and other judges. They
  cannot hide facts the model already knows.
- **No causal claim about GPT.** Progressive disclosure identifies where
  the Cursor panel's warrant changes. It does not prove why GPT-5.4 or
  GPT-5.4-mini transitioned.
