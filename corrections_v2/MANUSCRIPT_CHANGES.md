Suggested corrected conclusion pending reruns:

> On the original 10,000 paired FEVER claims, gold evidence increased aggregate accuracy while 226 unique claims exhibited correct-to-wrong regressions. These behavioral results are unchanged by the diagnostic audit. Evidence reconstruction and taxonomy defects prevent the historical three-way adjudication shares from supporting the claimed prevalence and cross-family ordering of failure modes. Repaired packets and an explicit, descriptive taxonomy are available; corrected Stage C judgments and coauthor review are required before drawing revised diagnostic conclusions.
