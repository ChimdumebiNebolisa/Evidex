## Interpreting the numbers

The summary CSV supplies all four 10,000-decision model-condition groups. The combined 20,000-decision condition totals pool the two evaluated models over the same 10,000 claims. They do not double the number of distinct claims.

The 396 and 446 evidence-condition error counts include all incorrect decisions in those conditions. They are not paired correct-to-wrong regression counts. The later 226-claim regression cohort must not be described as a result documented by this April dossier.

The old file named `error_taxonomy_counts_balanced_10000_v1.csv` records classification-error directions. Its name does not make it equivalent to the later Grok/Claude mechanism taxonomy.

This archive preserves reported historical outputs. No model inference or full experimental reproduction was performed to assemble it, and no later statistical or adjudication results were inserted into the old files.
