## Decision

The fresh experiment's recorded benchmark results are reproducible. Independent reconstruction matches 92 frozen point estimates and descriptive confidence intervals: 58 for the full claim set and 34 for fresh outcomes within the separately reported historically selected cohort. These latter estimates are not a reanalysis of historical model responses. The full raw-record and preservation checks have status **PASS**: 180,000 requests and 720,008 inventoried artifacts checked. No numerical correction to frozen outputs is warranted by these checks.

The material interpretation issue is that a **benchmark-defined regression is not automatically a factual or evidential failure**. In the deliberately selected taxonomy sample, supplied evidence sometimes conflicts with the frozen reference, omits a necessary fact, or admits different readings. Those are AI-assisted review flags, not validated new gold labels. Retain all frozen scores and narrow any claim that every recurring regression demonstrates model failure. This limits the interpretation of individual cases without erasing the aggregate benchmark improvements.
