# Title options

Working title used in `main.tex`:

**Evidence Helps on Average, but Sometimes Hurts: Diagnosing Evidence-Induced Regressions in LLM Fact Verification**

## Candidates

1. **Evidence Helps on Average, but Sometimes Hurts: Diagnosing Evidence-Induced Regressions in LLM Fact Verification**  
   Chosen. States the aggregate/local tension and the diagnostic task without overclaiming a universal mechanism.

2. **When Designated Evidence Hurts: A Cross-Family Diagnosis of LLM Fact-Verification Regressions**  
   Stronger on the hurt phenomenon; slightly underplays the larger accuracy gain.

3. **Gold Evidence, Mixed Failures: Cross-Family Silver Adjudication of LLM Fact-Verification Regressions**  
   Accurate about method; denser jargon in the title.

4. **Judge-Family Sensitivity in Automated Failure-Mode Diagnosis of Evidence-Induced Regressions**  
   Foregrounds the methodological contribution; weaker as a first-look title.

5. **When Gold Evidence Hurts: Diagnosing Evidence-Induced Regressions in LLM Fact Verification**  
   Punchy; “hurts” can be read as the main result rather than a minority local effect.

No title claims novelty-of-first-kind. “Evidex” is omitted from the title so the phenomenon is foregrounded.
